    The road to the platinum

    About 200 hours, one playthrough, no missable trophies except one. Do the story first, then the 100% checklist, then the stranger strands and cleanup. Cheats disable trophies and saving — keep them off.

    The one trophy at risk: Positive Mental Attitude. Meditate with Lucia on the balcony at Jason’s house in Chapter 1, right after the mission Down to Earth — that meditation is gone after Chapter 1. Keep a Chapter 1 save to be safe.
